# Code of Conduct

This applies to every repository, issue, pull request, Discord channel and other space of the GregTech Infinity Ascension organisation.

## Be decent

- Treat people with respect. Disagree with ideas, never attack the person.
- No harassment, slurs, sexual content, doxxing or threats, in public or in private messages.
- Assume good faith. Many contributors write in a second language; English, Portuguese and Chinese are all welcome in discussion.
- Feedback on balance and design is welcome. Demanding, insulting or spamming maintainers is not.

## Contributions

- Only submit work you have the right to submit. Ports from other mods or packs must respect their licences and credit the source.
- Do not copy code or assets from projects whose licence does not allow it, including decompiled Mojang code.
- AI-assisted work is allowed under the [AI policy](AI_POLICY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and temporarily or permanently block anyone who breaks this code. Report problems privately to a maintainer on Discord.
